About the College

20+
Years of Excellence

Vinayak PG College, Chomu is a distinguished institution of higher learning affiliated to the University of Rajasthan, Jaipur. Established in 2005, the college has steadily grown into a centre of academic excellence, shaping thousands of graduates across the arts, science, and commerce streams.

Located in the historic town of Chomu, the college offers a vibrant campus life with modern facilities, dedicated faculty, and a robust curriculum designed to prepare students for the competitive world. Our commitment to holistic development—intellectual, physical, and moral—remains the cornerstone of the Vinayak vision.

Our Facilities

Modern infrastructure designed to foster academic achievement, creative growth, and physical well-being for every student on campus.

Science Labs · Practical Training

Admission 2025–2026

Vinayak PG College welcomes applications for all UG and PG programmes for the academic session 2025–26. Admissions are based on merit in qualifying examinations as per the University of Rajasthan norms.
